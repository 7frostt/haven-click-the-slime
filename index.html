Key Gameplay Mechanics

Anti-Spam Jump Physics

Slime leaps into the air with 7-frame animation and bounce-back physics. Clicks while airborne are ignored to reward rhythm.

Combo Frenzy 2X Boost

Reach 10 consecutive rhythmic clicks to trigger Frenzy Mode, doubling all coin gains for 7 intense seconds.

Quick Shop (Food & Drinks)

Instant single-use consumable buffs. Fruits multiply coins earned (+25% to +75%), while canned sodas reduce jump cooldown (-15% to -35%).

Exponential Progression

Mathematically tuned economic curve across 150 levels with a conclusive MAX milestone ending at Level 150.

Architecture & Project Hierarchy

Clean 3-layer architecture for hackathon readability and maintainability:

scripts/main.gd

Coordinator: Handles state, exponential formulas, level upgrades, jump tweening, and place transitions.

scripts/ui_manager.gd

UI Controller: Manages HUD cards, shop items, cooldown countdowns, and button state toggling.

scripts/feedback_manager.gd

Game Feel: Controls audio SFX playback, floating score popups, and squash-and-stretch button tweens.

assets/

Strictly partitioned folders for audio, backgrounds, fonts, shop items, and slime sprite frames.
